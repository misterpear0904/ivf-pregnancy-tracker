# 03 — QA Report: End-User Simulation & Clinical-Safety Audit
**Subagent GAMMA (End-User QA & Functionality Simulation) · Oct 23, 2026 · v1.0**
Audits: `01-clinical-protocol.md` (clinical source of truth v1.0) and `02-ux-blueprint.md` (Beta v1.0).
Persona embodied: "Maya," 34, 5w4d, day-5 blast FET carrying a boy, prior loss, PIO qHS, full-time work, iPhone SE (375px) + laptop, 2 a.m. Googler, health anxiety, allergic to condescension.

---

## 1. Executive verdict: **SHIP WITH FIXES**

This is the rare patient-facing spec where the architecture matches the clinical document instead of the other way around. The two-tier triage model is faithfully carried into the Safety modal, inline red-flag cards keep the "any spotting → call" rule intact, the missed-dose flow structurally cannot produce a double-dose instruction, and the "Log ≠ Worry" separation plus the "do not track nausea as a dashboard metric" rule (blueprint §12, echoing clinical doc L276) show real anxiety-design literacy, not vibes. The blueprint also gets the two most dangerous moments right on paper: the indeterminate 6-week scan ("thresholds are deliberately conservative… follow-up 7–14 days later is routine") and the missed PIO dose ("your clinic will tell you").

It is not shippable as-is. The blocking class of defects is the same elephant in several rooms: **the check-in captures only booleans and 0–10 sliders**, so severe one-sided pain, shoulder-tip pain, heavy bleeding, and clots — the exact Tier-2 triggers in clinical doc L216–L228 — can only enter the system as "cramps: true" / "spotting: true," which produces a Tier-1-shaped card. The woman having an ectopic is served a "call your clinic today" card with the ER criteria one disclosure deeper. The Safety modal saves that only if she thinks to open it; the check-in path does not. That's a P0. Secondary blockers: no `[VERIFY LOCALLY]`-linked location capture for 988/911 context, math/copy errors in shipped examples, and content gaps (pelvic rest, missed NIPT/no-call, 2 a.m. entry) that this specific persona *will* hit in week one. Fix the listed P0/P1s and this ships.

## 3. The 12 scenario walkthroughs (think-aloud, patient voice)

**S1 — First open, no data, onboarding · PASS (minor)**
Cold-opens to 3-step onboarding with [Explore demo mode] prominent. "Enter your transfer date" is the only required thing; the hint "EDD = transfer + 261 days" educates without lecturing. She taps demo mode first, pokes everything, sees "Demo mode — not your data" banner. *Voice: "Oh good — I can snoop around without committing. Most apps demand my whole life first."* Defect D-15: demo-mode seeding spec exists but no demo-mode *Reset* is specified; if she starts a real profile later, does seeded demo data linger? Unspecified.

**S2 — 7:05 a.m., opens Today for PIO · FAIL (spec gap)**
The spec's example Focus card literally says **"Take your evening PIO at 8pm"** at a morning open. The focus-ranking rule §9.5 says "Daily med not yet taken *within window* → Take {med} now" — **"within window" is never defined** (D-03). Risk: at 7:05 a.m. with a 10 a.m. prenatal and 8 p.m. PIO, the app either nags for the 8 p.m. shot 13 hours early (alarming: "did I misremember my schedule?") or shows a focus action she can't act on for hours. Also: nothing specifies what the Focus card becomes after she marks done — the single most-run interaction has no defined end state (D-04). *Voice: "Wait — is my PIO supposed to be at night? Is this telling me to take it now? I'm shaking. I'll text my nurse instead. That's the app's failure mode: making me call a human to un-confuse it."*

**S3 — Misses morning PIO, realizes 2 p.m. · PASS with P1s**
Entry: "You missed a dose. This happens, and it's fixable." — exactly right tone. Question flow leads PIO to: "isn't something to guess at — your clinic will tell you to skip-and-resume or take it late. Never take a double dose. Never stop cold turkey." + [📞 Call clinic] [📞 After-hours] [Copy note to read]. At 2 p.m. the main-line CTA is correct and one tap; double-dose is structurally impossible; ending screen never dead-ends. *Voice: "'This happens' — thank god. Last time I missed one I cried in a bathroom."* But: (a) it routes to a phone call even in business hours without offering the **also-safest documented alternative** (clinical doc L69 explicitly permits "do the remaining day's dose on schedule" as a clinic-sanctioned skip path — the app's step-3 flow makes the call feel mandatory before answers are available, D-05); (b) clinically doc L67 says for *same-day realization of ordinary meds* "take as soon as remembered" — Blueprint Branch B says >8h = "skip today's" without acknowledging clinics differ; acceptable-but-should-cite (D-06).

**S4 — Light brown spotting after wiping · PASS (the best moment in the spec)**
She taps ☐ spotting in Check-in → inline card, verbatim: "*Most first-trimester bleeding isn't a bad sign — but the clinic would still like a call, even if you're sure it's nothing.*" Both verbs present: [📞 Call clinic] [Log & mention at next visit]. No false reassurance ("isn't a bad sign" is immediately followed by "still like a call"), no alarmism, phone is real buttons not a "learn more" link. *Voice: "It told me not to panic AND not to blow it off, in the same breath. That's the whole thing I needed."* Caveat D-02 below: heavy bleeding entering via the same checkbox gets this same Tier-1 card.

**S5 — Severe one-sided pelvic pain + shoulder-tip pain · FAIL (P0)**
She does what anxious patients do: opens Check-in, taps ☐ cramps. The spec's inline cramps card (blueprint L408) says "*Mild, on-and-off, both-sided cramps are common. If pain is severe, one-sided, or with shoulder-tip pain, go to the ER.*" — but the **input** that produced it has no severity, sidedness, or shoulder-tip control, so the routing engine can't know which case she's in; the default rendering is the Tier-1 "Call your clinic" emphasis with "go to the ER" inside a sentence she has to read while panicking. §9's rule that Tier-2 triggers "render the Safety modal with Tier 2 scrolled-into-view" can't fire because the Tier-2 trigger isn't representable in the data model (D-01). The tell (shoulder-tip pain — the classic internal-bleeding clue, clinical doc L228) is nowhere first-class; it's buried inside a prose sentence. *Voice at 3 a.m.: "My app said mild cramps are common. It literally does not know what I just told it. I Googled. We all know how that ends."* This is the single most important fix in the report.

**S6 — Logs "no symptoms," feels frightened · PASS**
Check-in "no verdicts" design + calm-line 3 ("Symptoms wax and wane…") + FAQ 3 ("plenty of healthy pregnancies have almost no symptoms… Don't use 'am I still nauseous?' as a health metric") + the crucial asymmetry correctly preserved (clinical doc L275: absence is not a sign; *sudden loss of established symptoms* is worth a call — the app must say the second half too, which FAQ 3 does). *Voice: "It's the first thing ever that told me feeling okay could mean things are okay."* Minor: the app should not passively accept a silent day with zero visible counter-message; the day's calm line is the only counterweight. P3.

**S7 — 6w2d scan, no heartbeat · PASS with copy fix**
Appointment card's "What it can't tell you" panel carries the exact therapeutic message from clinical doc L109–L111 (CRL ≥7mm / MSD ≥25mm are *diagnostic*; smaller-with-no-heartbeat is *suspicious, not diagnostic* → repeat 7–14 days). It avoids "failure/abnormal" per the rule table. *Voice: "'Routine, not a worry' — don't tell me not to worry, I'm going to worry; tell me the thresholds were built so a healthy pregnancy is never declared failed. Oh. It says that too."* Two fixes: "routine, not a worry" flirts with forced positivity (D-09); the **waiting window plan** (scanxiety rituals, clinic-doc L298–L301: distraction blocks, worry script, pre-decided plan) is completely absent from the post-scan state (D-14) — the 7–14 day gap is the single highest-anxiety interval of the whole pregnancy and the app currently has nothing to say into it.

**S8 — NIPT "boy" at 10w · PASS with P1 gap**
FAQ 7 is accurate and calm: "reads Y-DNA from the placenta; accuracy from 10 weeks is roughly 98–99%, but it's a screen, not a certainty," and it correctly promises the anatomy scan as the confirmatory look. No 100% overpromise anywhere. The subtle nuance is even preserved elsewhere (male call = Y reads present; female = absence — clinical doc L127). *Voice: "98–99 and a scan to confirm. Fine. That's a number I can work with."* Gap D-13: **no-call / low fetal fraction** (~1–5%, clinical doc L124/L129) has no path — and it's precisely the result that produces a 2 a.m. panic; vanishing-twin caveat (L125) also absent. This persona has a day-5 single blast but her history makes "what if the result is weird" a live fear.

**S9 — Graduation to OB at 9w · PASS with P2s**
Celebration copy is measured ("Your RE handed the reins to your OB" — no firework-drenched forced cheer). Export/Print produces the OB handout including the "My actual protocol" fill-in table (clinical doc L349–L367, carried verbatim). The milestone schedule carries the first prenatal OB visit. Gaps: nothing at 9w says *when* graduation happens for *her* (clinic-variation table L343 says ask "what week do you graduate, and what do you send to the OB?" — that's a great FAQ line with no FAQ), and there's no emotional copy for the "abandoned by my clinic" feeling, which every pregnancy-after-loss patient reports. Records *transfer logistics* (release forms, portal handoff) are outside scope but one FAQ line would land. D-16, D-10.

**S10 — Week-12 boy card · PASS with P2**
The boy-milestone toggle carries SRY ~w6–7, testosterone ramp 8–12, anatomy confirm 18–22 — all faithful to clinical doc L313–L318/L324. Week-12 card contents are unspecified in the blueprint (only the toggle list is), and the two adjacent facts must not run together wrongly: doc L318 says external genitalia are "largely formed" by ~12–13w, but doc L137/L307 explicitly says **not reliably distinguishable on ultrasound until ~14w and never via a "nub" guess by non-professionals**. The week-12 card *must* state "you cannot see sex on a scan this week — NIPT is where boy comes from" or impatient relatives will request a 12-week "gender scan" and the app will have implied one is meaningful. D-15 (week-12 card spec gap).

**S11 — 3 a.m., "is this normal?" panic · PASS (the spec's second-best moment)**
Safety pill is persistent on the nav → 1 tap, anywhere, any state. Modal Tier 2 is on top (correct order — worst case is reachable without scrolling), includes 988 for mental-health emergencies and the full phone trio on every inline red-flag card ("never only a 'learn more' href without a call button"). Copy is verbs, not verdicts. *Voice: "One tap. The phone numbers are buttons. It doesn't ask me how I feel first. Ships."* Caveat: on a 320×667pt SE the nav is 4 tabs + pill + (⚙ + 📞 Clinic in header) — 7 targets; D-07. Also 988/911 buttons should open a confirm or direct tel: — spec says tel: stub; untested on file:// iOS (D-17).

**S12 — Returns after 5 days away · FAIL (spec gap)**
Streak logic says gaps render as "You did 4 days, then a rest day. Back to it gently." — with 5 *silent* days, "a rest day" is a euphemism that rings false, and there is **no defined re-entry state at all**: what does Today show? Does it back-fill missed log days (which reads as an accusation)? Does the missed-dose handler fire for 5 days of unlogged meds × the >24h rule (which must — the clinical doc is unambiguous that >24h off progesterone is a call-able event, L70)? Nothing specifies: (a) re-entry message, (b) back-fill policy for doses, (c) whether 5 unlogged days of PIO triggers the missed-dose obligation. D-08. *Voice: "I hid from the app because I was depressed for five days. If I open it and it waves 40 missed things at me I will delete it. If it pretends nothing happened while my PIO ran out five days ago, it's negligent."* The right design: gentle re-entry ("Welcome back") *plus* the refill/last-dose truth surfaced once, non-judgmentally.

## 5. Top 10 anxiety-reduction changes (safe to ship)

1. **Make the severity gate real** (D-01/D-02): any "cramps/spotting" rows → second question "how bad / one-sided / shoulder-tip / heavy?" and let the *data* pick the tier, not her reading comprehension at panic speed.
2. **Add the waiting-window plan** (D-14) to every appointment card: pre-decided plan, worry script, distraction blocks, "scan partner" — straight from doc L298–L301. Nothing reduces scanxiety like a rehearsed script.
3. **Re-entry state** (D-08): absence handled with one honest, soft card (last-dose truth + refill + no guilt) rather than 5 days of silent nothing or a wave of red.
4. **Who-to-call and when**, live, per-calendar: after-hours availability is real in the phone trio — display the *currently appropriate* line ("After 5 p.m., use the after-hours line first") so she never has to decide which number at 3 a.m.
5. **Copy fixes** (D-09/D-12/D-10): delete every "don't worry"-shaped sentence; replace each with the more-specific reassurance ("thresholds built so a healthy pregnancy is never declared failed") — reassuring and true beats comforting and vague.
6. **Rehearse the missed-PIO call** (D-05): show what the clinic will likely say in a `<details>` *before* the call button, so calling feels bounded.
7. **On the Focus card after "done"** (D-04): do not refill her to-do anxiety; return to the calm line and stop. The rewarded behavior of a finished day is *stopping*, which is an anti-anxiety feature in itself.
8. **Never show streaks when anxiety ≥8** (existing rule is good) — but also never compute a streak from *scheduled* anything while in re-entry (D-08) or demo; only from confirmed behavior.
9. **Calmer default-light mode, but dark mode off-default strengthens the margins** where 2 a.m. use actually happens (a dark-retina at 3 a.m. is a real factor); expedite the dark-mode toggle from optional to v1.
10. **FAQ additions** (D-13/D-16): no-call NIPT + graduation handoff — the two content gaps most likely to produce 2 a.m. panic searches.
